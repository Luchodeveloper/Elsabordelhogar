## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, craftsmanship, and intimacy of an artisanal family bakery. The aesthetic centers on the sensory memories of fresh baking: warm flour dusting, golden crusts, rich roasted cacao, and genuine hospitality. It avoids sterile corporate polish in favor of a tactile, welcoming, and deeply comforting digital storefront.

The visual direction merges **Warm Editorial Craft** with soft, pillowy interfaces. Surfaces feel soft and organic rather than mechanical, using generous radii, airy spacing, and golden ambient glows that mirror late-morning sunlight streaming into a kitchen. Typography balances the expressive grace of a heritage serif with the crisp legibility of modern geometric sans. The atmosphere feels generous, trustworthy, and distinctly handcrafted.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the elemental ingredients of baking—stone-ground grains, caramelized sugars, natural honey, and rich cocoa beans.

- **Primary (`#A67C52` - Golden Honey):** The action and artisanal anchor. Applied to primary calls to action, active navigation markers, key icons, and focal accents.
- **Secondary (`#5A3F2B` - Baked Crust):** The foundation of trust, heritage, and structural balance. Used for primary typography, authoritative headers, strong borders, and high-emphasis icons.
- **Tertiary (`#D8A47F` - Spun Sugar):** A soft, roasted pastry tone used for delicate badges, secondary state highlights, and subtle textural separators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7EADC` - Warm Flour):** The atmospheric canvas tone. Softens ocular strain, provides a comforting warmth, and differentiates the interface from standard cold white applications.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Cream):** Distinct, elevated cards, bottom sheets, and modal surfaces that lift off the canvas with warm clarity.
- **Subtle Fill (`#EFE2D2` - Wheat Mist):** Secondary card fills, muted tags, disabled states, and subtle inset backgrounds.
- **Direct Messaging Accent (`#25D366` - Hearth Order WhatsApp):** Reserved strictly for WhatsApp ordering and direct conversational commerce touchpoints, styled with warm ambient glows to harmonize with the earth tones.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Playfair Display** (a classic editorial serif with soft transitions and expressive curves) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a friendly, highly legible modern geometric sans).

- **Playfair Display** is applied exclusively to high-level headers, section titles, hero quotes, and featured flavor profiles. It communicates time-honored baking traditions and artisanal pride.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** manages content density, product ingredient descriptions, pricing, navigation, forms, and order receipts. Its wide apertures and soft curves provide frictionless reading even at compact mobile dimensions.
- Use italicized cuts of Playfair Display sparingly for bakery signatures, story excerpts, and baker's notes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt rhythmic grid within a flexible container system that maximizes content appetite appeal while keeping purchasing pathways straightforward.

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px):** Single-column stack with dynamic full-width edge cards, pinned bottom order summary sheets, 1rem screen margins, and minimum 48px touch targets for rapid one-handed navigation.
- **Tablet Viewports (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with 1.5rem gutters, ideal for split browse-and-inspect bakery catalog modes.
- **Desktop Viewports (> 1024px):** 12-column responsive layout capped at a max-width of 1280px, flanked by warm flour-toned negative margins. Allows two-column splits (e.g., artisanal product storytelling on the left, customization and ordering panels on the right).
- Spacing embraces generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl`) between distinct categories, reflecting the unhurried care of artisanal preparation.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids cold digital drop shadows, instead utilizing warm, ambient, sunlit diffusions tinted with roasted cocoa and honey tones (`#5A3F2B`).

- **Flat / Ground Level (0dp):** Canvas `#F7EADC` baseline for static page backgrounds and structural dividers.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Lift):** Cards, ingredients tiles, and list containers rest on pure cream (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating):** Hovered pastry cards, dropdown menus, and cart counters: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Pinned Actions):** Sticky WhatsApp order triggers, custom cake configurators, and cart trays: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -6px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.08)`.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Low-contrast organic rims using `rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.08)` to frame cream surfaces without harsh segmentation.

## Shapes

The geometry of this design system draws directly from risen dough, rounded loaf tins, and hand-molded pastries. There are no sharp, abrasive edges.

- Buttons, chips, search fields, and quantity toggles use pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile quality.
- Product cards, imagery masks, and recipe modals employ deep, flowing radii (`rounded-2xl` at 1.5rem to `rounded-3xl` at 2rem).
- Badges and specialty stamp ribbons use organic pill contours or smooth, gentle arches that hint at artisan packaging seals and kraft gift tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Bakery Button:** Solid `#A67C52` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded with `12px 24px`. On hover: lifts 1px with a warmer amber tone (`#966E45`) and subtle ambient shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface cream `#FFFFFF` background, solid 1.5px `#5A3F2B` border, `#5A3F2B` typography.
- **WhatsApp Direct Order Button:** Dedicated high-priority button utilizing `#25D366` background, crisp white label, integrated brand icon, and an ambient green-honey glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.25)`). Ideal for immediate home delivery requests.

### Cards
- **Pastry Showcase Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `rounded-3xl` corners, 1px perimeter stroke of `rgba(90, 63, 43, 0.08)`. Top image features an organic rounded mask with zero bleed at top corners. Card footer contains baked brown typography (`#5A3F2B`), honey pricing highlight (`#A67C52`), and an intuitive circular quick-add icon button.

### Chips & Badges
- **Artisan Badges (e.g., "Masa Madre", "Recién Horneado", "100% Mantequilla"):** Styled as soft badges with `#EFE2D2` background, `#5A3F2B` label typography, `rounded-full`, and micro-paddings (`4px 12px`).
- **Seasonal Highlight Tags:** Background of `#D8A47F` with white typography for limited-batch goods.

### Form Inputs & Quantities
- **Text & Order Inputs:** Soft cream fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px` border in `#EFE2D2`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-2xl` depending on single-line vs. multi-line field. Focus state transitions smoothly to `#A67C52` border with a soft warm focus ring (`rgba(166, 124, 82, 0.20)`).
- **Steppers & Quantity Selectors:** Pill-shaped unified element with neutral background (`#EFE2D2`), containing deep baked brown (`#5A3F2B`) decrement/increment buttons framing an active numerical count.

### Lists & Menu Schedules
- **Baking Schedule & Flavor Lists:** Borderless white list rows separated by `12px` gaps over the warm canvas, featuring thumbnail avatars, rich descriptive metadata, and availability badges indicating morning vs. afternoon fresh batches.
